## Energy Savings

Smart thermostats save an average of **$50-$150 per year** on heating and cooling by:
- Learning your schedule and adjusting automatically
- Detecting when you're away (geofencing)
- Optimizing based on weather forecasts
- Providing energy reports to change behavior

## Installation Tips

### DIY Installation (Most Models)
1. Turn off HVAC at breaker
2. Remove old thermostat, note wire labels
3. Mount new thermostat base plate
4. Connect wires to matching terminals
5. Attach display, power on at breaker
6. Follow app setup wizard

### When You Need a Pro
- No C-wire (common wire) — some systems need one added
- High-voltage systems (baseboard heaters)
- Multi-zone or heat pump systems with complex wiring
- If you see thick wires (line voltage, not low voltage)

## Compatibility Check

Before buying, verify your system:
- **Works with**: central AC, gas/oil/electric furnace, heat pump, radiant
- **May not work with**: 110/240V baseboard, gravity furnace, some multi-zone

## Maximize Your Smart Thermostat

1. **Use schedules** — even auto-learning thermostats benefit from initial programming
2. **Set eco temperatures** — reasonable away temps save more than extreme settings
3. **Add room sensors** — balance hot/cold rooms
4. **Enable geofencing** — auto-away when everyone leaves
5. **Check reports monthly** — identify waste patterns
