# HVAC Energy Efficiency Guide

Practical tips to reduce your heating and cooling costs by 20-40%.

## Quick Wins (Free or Low Cost)

### 1. Thermostat Management
- Set to 78°F in summer, 68°F in winter
- Use programmable schedules — save 10% annually
- Smart thermostats learn your patterns and optimize automatically

### 2. Air Filter Maintenance
- Replace every 1-3 months (monthly during peak use)
- Dirty filters force the system to work 15% harder
- MERV 8-11 filters balance efficiency and air quality

### 3. Seal Air Leaks
- Weatherstrip doors and windows
- Seal ductwork joints with mastic (not duct tape)
- Insulate attic access panels
- Caulk around pipes and wires entering walls

### 4. Optimize Airflow
- Keep vents open and unblocked
- Don't close more than 20% of registers
- Use ceiling fans (counterclockwise in summer)
- Keep outdoor unit clear (2ft clearance all sides)

## Moderate Investments ($200-$2,000)

### Duct Sealing
Leaky ducts waste 20-30% of conditioned air. Professional [duct cleaning and sealing](https://ac-repair.today/services/duct-cleaning/) pays for itself in 1-2 years.

### Attic Insulation
Adding insulation to R-38 or higher reduces cooling load by 10-15%.

### Window Treatments
- Solar film reduces heat gain 30-50%
- Cellular shades provide R-4 insulation
- Plant shade trees on south/west sides

## Major Upgrades ($3,000-$15,000)

### High-Efficiency HVAC
- Look for SEER 16+ ratings (vs old 10 SEER units)
- Variable-speed compressors save 30-40%
- Heat pumps are more efficient than traditional AC in moderate climates

### Smart Zoning
- Zone systems cool only occupied areas
- Reduce energy use by 30%
- Pair with smart thermostats for maximum savings
